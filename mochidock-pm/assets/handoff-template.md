## Claim classification

Use these labels wherever ambiguity is possible:

- **Verified fact:** confirmed from current project evidence.
- **Documented agreement:** recorded as user-approved but not independently testable.
- **Inference:** reasoned from available evidence and not yet verified.
- **Recommendation:** proposed next action, not approved scope.
- **User decision needed:** cannot proceed safely without confirmation.
