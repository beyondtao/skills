# German Learning

## Purpose

Support native Chinese speakers in learning German with clear explanations, corrections, practice, and review.

## Current Scope

Provide focused assistance based on the learner's request without assuming a specific proficiency level. This Skill is still being developed incrementally.

## Future Development

Expand the learning guidance and supporting resources gradually as requirements become clear. Do not establish a complete teaching system at this stage.
